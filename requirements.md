# Requirements Document – AI Grievance Platform for Bharat

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this system is to provide a unified digital platform that enables citizens to easily submit, track, and escalate public grievances while reducing complexity, fragmentation, and manual effort in the grievance redressal process.

---

## 2. Problem Definition

Current grievance redressal systems in India are fragmented across multiple portals and departments. Citizens often face difficulties in identifying the correct platform, filling complex forms, and tracking complaint status. This leads to delayed resolutions, low transparency, and reduced trust in governance systems.

---

## 3. Target Users

- Citizens from rural, semi-urban, and urban regions
- Users with limited digital literacy
- Community members reporting shared public issues
- Government officials and administrators (for monitoring and resolution)

---

## 4. Functional Requirements

### 4.1 Grievance Submission
- The system shall allow users to submit grievances through a web-based interface.
- The system shall support text, voice, and image-based inputs.
- The system shall capture location information for grievances where applicable.

### 4.2 AI-Assisted Processing
- The system shall automatically analyze submitted grievances.
- The system shall classify grievances by category and urgency.
- The system shall generate structured complaint summaries from unstructured inputs.

### 4.3 Grievance Routing
- The system shall map grievances to the appropriate department or authority.
- The system shall support reassignment in case of incorrect routing.

### 4.4 Grievance Tracking
- The system shall assign a unique identifier to each grievance.
- The system shall allow users to track grievance status through the platform.
- The system shall maintain a clear grievance lifecycle.

### 4.5 Escalation & Visibility
- The system shall allow community upvoting for recurring or widespread issues.
- The system shall highlight high-impact grievances for administrative review.

---

## 5. Non-Functional Requirements

### 5.1 Usability
- The system shall be simple and intuitive to use.
- The interface shall minimize user input complexity.

### 5.2 Reliability
- The system shall ensure consistent grievance state tracking.
- The system shall prevent loss of grievance data.

### 5.3 Performance
- The system shall handle multiple grievance submissions without degradation.
- The system shall process inputs within acceptable response times.

### 5.4 Security & Privacy
- The system shall protect user data and submitted content.
- The system shall restrict administrative access using role-based controls.

---

## 6. Out of Scope

- Direct resolution of grievances by the platform
- Replacement of existing government grievance portals
- Legal enforcement or decision-making authority
- Manual verification of all AI outputs

---

## 7. Assumptions & Constraints

- The platform acts as a facilitation and automation layer.
- Final resolution authority remains with government departments.
- The initial implementation focuses on core grievance categories.

---

## 8. Success Criteria

- Successful submission and tracking of grievances
- Accurate AI-based classification and routing
- Clear visibility of grievance status to users
- Demonstrable end-to-end workflow during hackathon demo

---

## 9. Summary

This requirements document defines the functional and non-functional expectations of the AI Grievance Platform. It ensures clarity of scope, user needs, and system responsibilities while keeping the solution realistic and achievable within a hackathon environment.
